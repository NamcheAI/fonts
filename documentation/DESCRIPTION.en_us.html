Namche Shadow Sans is a display-oriented sans-serif based on Vercel's Geist Sans. Michael Marte designed its rounded inner-corner treatment for Ruhm GmbH. The companion Namche Shadow Mono and Namche Shadow Pixel families currently preserve the outlines and metrics of Geist Mono and Geist Pixel so they can evolve independently in future releases.

The Namche Shadow derivative is owned by BTLG Holding GmbH and was developed in collaboration with Ruhm GmbH.

Geist was created by Vercel in collaboration with Basement Studio, Andrés Briganti, Mateo Zaragoza, and its other contributors. Namche Shadow Sans retains their copyright notices and is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
