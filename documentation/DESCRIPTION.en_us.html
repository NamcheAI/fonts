Namche Shadow Sans is a display-oriented sans-serif based on Vercel's Geist Sans. Michael Marte designed its rounded inner-corner treatment for Ruhm etc.. The companion Namche Shadow Mono remains outline-identical to Geist Mono. Namche Shadow Pixel retains Geist's geometry and accepts only focused, reviewed additions and shaping corrections.

The Namche Shadow derivative is owned by BTLG Holding GmbH and was developed in collaboration with Ruhm etc.

Geist was created by Vercel in collaboration with Basement Studio, Andrés Briganti, Mateo Zaragoza, and its other contributors. Namche Shadow Sans retains their copyright notices and is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
