Namche Shadow Sans adapts the Geist family for Namche. Its primary sans family uses a custom rounded inner-corner treatment designed by Michael Marte for Ruhm GmbH. The derivative is owned by BTLG Holding GmbH. Mono and Pixel are deliberately outline-identical renamed derivatives of Geist Mono and Geist Pixel in this initial release.

The project is based on Vercel's Geist repository and keeps its build layout, original authorship, and SIL Open Font License. Complete authorship and contribution records are included with the source.
